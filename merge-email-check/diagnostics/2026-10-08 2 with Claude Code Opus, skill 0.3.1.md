## Fix these first

1. **Draw the missing frames: Email 1's desktop frame, and both frames for Email 2 to 4 and P2 Core nurture** (EM-01, Must). Every reader gets one of these layouts, and an empty frame can't be checked or built. Examples: [Email 1 desktop](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=2-99), [Email 2 mobile](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=2-103).
2. **Add MERGE's postal address to the footer, and say in a Link or CTA note where the unsubscribe link goes** (EM-28, Must). Marketing email law requires the address and a working unsubscribe. Examples: [footer](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=28-96).
3. **Give the footer a solid green background color behind its image** (EM-10, Must). With images off, which is the default for most Outlook readers at work, the URL and Unsubscribe text become white on white. Examples: [footer](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=28-96).
4. **Add alt text or a decorative note to each image** (EM-06, Must). Screen readers and images-off readers otherwise get nothing, or a file name. Mark the hero photo decorative if you agree with the suggestion on it. Examples: [hero photo](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=32-13), [footer](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=28-96).
5. **Raise the contrast of "Unsubscribe" and the URL in the footer** (EM-19, Must). At about 2.9 to 1, the 10px Unsubscribe text is hard to read for people with low vision. A darker green behind it, or larger and bolder text, would fix it. Examples: [footer](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=28-96).
6. **Make the footer links at least 24px tall, and 44px to meet the house size** (EM-21, Must). Small targets are easy to miss on a phone. Examples: [footer](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=28-96).
7. **Underline "www.mergeworld.com" and "Unsubscribe"** (EM-22, Must). Without an underline, readers who can't see color differences can't tell they're links. Examples: [footer](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=28-96).
8. **Write a preheader and show it as a text layer named "preheader"** (EM-27, Should). Otherwise the inbox shows the first words it finds, here "FPO Series Name". Examples: [subject line note](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=27-16).
9. **Put a background color behind each image** (EM-11, Should). That way the layout holds its shape with images off. Examples: [hero photo](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=32-13).
10. **Name a web-safe fallback for Fraunces and Inter** (EM-17, Should). Outlook and Gmail don't load these fonts, so the developer needs to know what to show instead. Examples: [2:100](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=2-100).
11. **Add a Mobile behavior note to each module** (EM-03, Should). The build needs to know how each module stacks or hides, which saves a correction later. Examples: [Group 1](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=33-37), [CTA Button](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=32-25).
12. **Add a Dark mode note to each image** (EM-12, Should). Gmail and Outlook recolor around images, so the build needs to know what each one does. Examples: [hero photo](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=32-13).
13. **Change the pure white background to an off-white** (EM-15, Should). Pure white flips harshly in some dark-mode apps. Examples: [2:100](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=2-100).
14. **Set JPG or PNG export on each image** (EM-29, Should). The developer then gets the right files without guessing. Examples: [hero photo](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=32-13).

The header reads "FPO Series Name" ([header](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=33-111)), "Here's what that looks like" has no closing punctuation, "has been to AI to the same old model" reads as a typo, and "judgement" should be "judgment" in US English ([2:100](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=2-100)). ## Checked only in the build

This design check can't see the HTML or the send: its weight against Gmail's clip at about 102KB, Outlook-only code, the `alt` attributes, the dark-mode code, tracked links, and how each mail app renders. Check those in the build and on a real test send, with images off, from a mailbox that isn't on any safe-sender list.
