## Fix these first

We've ranked these Must first, then Should, then Could, and within each rank by how many readers they reach.

1. **Add the sender's postal address to Faults A's footer, and a Link or CTA note on its unsubscribe saying the developer sets the link** (EM-28, Must). Email law requires both, so the email can't be sent without them. Examples: [footer](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-65), [unsubscribe](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-68).
2. **Give Faults A's hero a dark background color behind the photo, so the white headline reads with images off** (EM-10, Must). Most Outlook readers at work see the email with images blocked, and right now the headline vanishes for them. Examples: [headline](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-8), [hero image](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-7).
3. **Rebuild the "Shop now" button and the banner offer in Faults A as live text** (EM-08, Must). With images off, the main button and the offer disappear, and screen readers get only alt text. Examples: [button](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-26), [banner](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-19).
4. **Darken Faults A's legal text to at least 4.5 to 1** (EM-19, Must). At 2.61 to 1, readers with low vision can't read it, and WCAG 2.2 AA requires it. Examples: [desktop](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-66), [mobile](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-134).
5. **Draw Faults B's mobile frame and fill in Faults C's empty desktop frame** (EM-01, Must). Without both, the developer has to guess how each email looks on the other screen size. Examples: [Faults B](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=8-5), [Faults C desktop](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=8-39).
6. **Make Faults A's "See the event calendar" link at least 24px tall, and the "Book a demo" button 44px** (EM-21, Must). Small targets are hard to tap on a phone. Examples: [link](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-62), [button](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-57).
7. **Underline Faults A's "Click here" and "Read more" links** (EM-22, Must). Readers who can't tell colors apart won't see them as links otherwise. Examples: [Click here](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-11), [Read more](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-61).
8. **Give Faults A's ghost button an edge of at least 3 to 1, and darken the gray calendar icon** (EM-20, Must). At 1.36 to 1 the button's shape is hard to see. Examples: [ghost button](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-59), [calendar](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-37).

Examples: [photo 1](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-47), [photo 2](https://www.figma.com/design/pgpRQNF2ey2fXl3lMS9D2O/?node-id=6-48).
