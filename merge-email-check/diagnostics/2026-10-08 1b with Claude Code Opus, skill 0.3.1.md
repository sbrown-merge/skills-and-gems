# Email check: Adobe Elevate email: projected worst cases

This email isn't ready to build from yet. Four Must checks fail or only partly pass, and the first thing to fix is the links: four of the five text links aren't underlined, so readers who can't tell blue from gray won't find them. The footer's gray small print also fails contrast in both light and dark mode.

Checked 2026-10-08 with merge-email-check 0.3.1. Scope: 110:2. Emails: Adobe Elevate email: projected worst cases. House numbers: MERGE's.

## Fix these first

1. **Underline every text link** (EM-22, Must). Four of the five links are told apart by color alone, so readers with color blindness, and anyone reading in bright light, can miss them. Examples: [Read now](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-14), [this link](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-26), [unsubscribe](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-32).
2. **Darken the footer gray and the link blue on the gray card and footer** (EM-19, Must). The legal text is 2.75 to 1 in light mode and 3.93 to 1 in dark, and the blue is 4.17 to 1, all under WCAG's 4.5 to 1, so readers with low vision can't read the unsubscribe and legal lines. Examples: [footer text](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-32), [Go to Marketo Engage](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-18), [dark footer text](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-61).
3. **Make the links at least 24px tall, and 44px for the main actions** (EM-21, Must). On a phone, "View in browser" at 16px and the 26px CTAs are hard to hit with a thumb. Examples: [View in browser](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-33), [Read now](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-14).
4. **Add a Link or CTA note to the unsubscribe link, and add a preferences or privacy link** (EM-28, Must). The developer needs to know where the unsubscribe goes, because email law requires it to work. Examples: [footer](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-32).
5. **Write a preheader and show it in the design** (EM-27, Should). Without one, the inbox preview shows whatever text comes first, which in Outlook is the blocked-image message. Add a text layer named "preheader".
6. **Make one action the primary CTA, as a button** (EM-26, Should). Two different text links compete, and neither stands out with images off. Examples: [Read now](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-14), [Go to Marketo Engage](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-18).
7. **Bring the main message and a lead into the CTA higher** (EM-04, Should). With images on, the 400px hero pushes the headline to y 542, below the preview area. Examples: [hero](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-37), [H1](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-38).
8. **Give every image a background color** (EM-11, Should). Outlook readers at work see empty white boxes where the images should be, so a color matched to each image keeps the layout's shape. Examples: [hero](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-37), [module image](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-40).
9. **Add a reversed logo for dark mode** (EM-13, Should). The red wordmark stays unchanged on the dark background in both dark frames. Examples: [header logo](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-36), [footer logo](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-59).
10. **Change the main background from pure white to a near-white** (EM-15, Should). Mail apps that recolor for dark mode treat pure white most harshly. Example: [email background](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-6).
11. **Rewrite "Read now" and "this link" to say where they go** (EM-24, Should). Screen reader users often jump from link to link, and these don't make sense on their own. Examples: [Read now](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-14), [this link](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-26).
12. **Keep words out of the module image, or treat them as decoration** (EM-09, Should). The page title in the screenshot disappears with images off and can't be read by screen readers. Examples: [module image](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-40).
13. **Move the mobile frame to the left of the desktop frames** (EM-02, Should). Example: [mobile frame](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-63).
14. **Set JPG or PNG export on each image** (EM-29, Should). It saves a build correction. Example: [hero](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=110-37).

## Checked only in the build

This design check can't see the HTML or the send: its weight against Gmail's clip at about 102KB, Outlook-only code, the `alt` attributes, the dark-mode code, tracked links, and how each mail app renders. Check those in the build and on a real test send, with images off, from a mailbox that isn't on any safe-sender list.

## What couldn't be checked

EM-03, EM-06, EM-07, EM-12, EM-23 and EM-25 couldn't be checked because the file uses none of the eight note kinds (script 01 read 0 notes). Adding Dev Mode annotations in the categories Alt text, Decorative image, Heading level, Link or CTA, Dark mode, Dynamic content, Mobile behavior and Content model field would let these checks run.
