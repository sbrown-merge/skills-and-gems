# Email check: Adobe Elevate email: why it works

This email isn't ready to build yet. Four Must fixes stand out: most text links have no underline, the footer and one dark-mode heading fail contrast, the links are too small to tap easily, and the unsubscribe link has no note saying where it goes. Start by underlining the text links and darkening the footer gray, because those two fixes help every reader.

Checked 2026-10-08 with merge-email-check 0.3.1. Scope: 106:6. Emails: Adobe Elevate email: why it works. House numbers: MERGE's.

## Fix these first

1. **Underline every text link** (EM-22, Must). Readers who can't tell blue from black, and anyone printing or reading in a recolored dark mode, can't find a link shown by color alone. Examples: [Read now](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-16), [this link](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-28), [unsubscribe](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-33).
2. **Darken the footer gray and the blue on the gray card, and keep the "Try it" heading dark in dark mode** (EM-19, Must). At 2.75 to 1 the footer text is hard to read for many people, and at 1.09 to 1 the dark-mode "Try it" heading disappears. Use about #707070 or darker on #f5f5f5 for the footer, and a slightly darker blue on the card. Examples: [footer text](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-32), [Go to Marketo Engage](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-20), [dark-mode Try it](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-47).
3. **Make each link at least 44px tall to tap** (EM-21, Must). On a phone, 16px and 26px links are easy to miss or mis-tap, and the 16px "View in browser" is under WCAG's 24px minimum. Add padding around the links, or turn the main action into a button. Examples: [View in browser](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-34), [Read now](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-16).
4. **Add a Link or CTA note to the unsubscribe saying where it goes, or that the developer sets it** (EM-28, Must). Email law needs a working unsubscribe, and the note keeps it from being missed in the build. A privacy link and a preferences link are worth adding too. Example: [unsubscribe](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-33).
5. **Move the headline, or a short lead-in to the action, into the first 300px** (EM-04, Should). The preview pane and the first screen on a phone show only the logo and the hero, so readers see nothing to read or do. A shorter hero would bring the headline up. Example: [hero image](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-2).
6. **Write a preheader and show it as a text layer named "preheader"** (EM-27, Should). Without one, inboxes fill the preview line with whatever text comes first. Example: [desktop frame](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-11).
7. **Choose one primary action and make it a button** (EM-26, Should). Two equal text links split attention, and neither stands out as the thing to do. Examples: [Read now](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-16), [Go to Marketo Engage](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-20).
8. **Give each image a background color** (EM-11, Should). With images blocked in Outlook, a color in their place keeps the layout from looking broken. Examples: [hero image](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-2), [module image](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-5).
9. **Keep the module image's words out of the image, or treat the image as decorative** (EM-09, Should). Words inside an image vanish with images off, and screen readers can't read them. Example: [module image](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-5).
10. **Name a web-safe fallback for the brand font** (EM-17, Should). Many mail apps, Outlook among them, can't load Adobe Clean or Source Sans 3, so the build needs to know what to show instead, such as Arial. Example: [font note](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-35).
11. **Add a reversed logo for dark mode** (EM-13, Should). Our policy swaps only the logo in dark mode, and the dark frame still shows the light-mode logo. Example: [dark-mode logo](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-38).
12. **Use an off-white and an off-black background instead of pure white and pure black** (EM-15, Should). Mail apps that recolor in dark mode handle near-white and near-black more predictably. Examples: [desktop light](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-11), [desktop dark](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-37).
13. **Rewrite "Read now" and "this link" so they say where they go** (EM-24, Should). Screen-reader users often jump from link to link, and "this link" means nothing out of context. Examples: [Read now](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-16), [this link](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-28).
14. **Set a JPG or PNG export on each image** (EM-29, Should). The developer then gets the right files without guessing. Example: [hero image](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-2).
15. **Move the phone frame to the left of the desktop frames** (EM-02, Should). Developers read the frames left to right, starting with mobile. Example: [phone frame](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-67).

## Problems no check covers

In the dark frame, "Go to Marketo Engage" turns from blue to black ([106:49](https://www.figma.com/design/Dqux2GL6tXD0boEEW3QCax/?node-id=106-49)), so the link no longer looks like a link in dark mode. We propose a check for the maintainer: "Links keep a distinct link color and underline in dark mode."

## Checked only in the build

This design check can't see the HTML or the send: its weight against Gmail's clip at about 102KB, Outlook-only code, the `alt` attributes, the dark-mode code, tracked links, and how each mail app renders. Check those in the build and on a real test send, with images off, from a mailbox that isn't on any safe-sender list.

## What couldn't be checked

EM-03, EM-06, EM-07, EM-12 and EM-23 couldn't be checked, because the file has no Dev Mode notes: script 01 read 0 annotations. Its numbered callouts are drawn on the canvas instead. To make these checks possible, add Dev Mode annotations in eight kinds: Alt text, Decorative image, Heading level, Link or CTA, Dark mode, Dynamic content, Mobile behavior and Content model field.
